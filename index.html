The Kearney Insider and the Online Board are two different things.

People mix these up, so here is the difference.

The Kearney Insider

A printed postcard that arrives in the mail.

Where your ad shows up
On a 6 x 11 inch card in 3,000 Kearney mailboxes.
Who else is on it
Other local businesses, but never one in your category.
How long it runs
One mailing per round.
Cost
Priced per round. Call or text me for the current rate.

The Online Board

A page of ads on this website.

Where your ad shows up
On the board page here, and on the deals page if you run an offer.
Who else is on it
Any local business. Categories are not exclusive online.
How long it runs
All month, for as many months as you want.
Cost
$25, $50, or $100 a month depending on size.
