About Colleen

Twenty five years of keeping other people's books clean, in banks, on a ranch, at a marketing agency and on a manufacturing floor. Clean Books is where all of that comes together.

Colleen McNeil
Owner, Clean Books Business Services, LLC
Kenesaw, Nebraska

How she got here

Colleen started in banking in 2001 at First National Bank, where she ran the cash services department and was responsible for balancing every teller drawer, vault and ATM in the building. Bank audits rated her department strong and low risk three years running. That is where the habit of balancing to the penny comes from.

From there she managed the office of a working ranch in northern California, kept the books for a marketing agency in Hastings, and spent 10 years in HVAC manufacturing as finance manager, where she untangled financial mapping across three accounting and MRP systems and eventually moved the whole company onto QuickBooks.

She opened Clean Books Business Services in 2015 to work directly with small business owners, and now does it full time. Her clients have included cattle and horse ranches, construction companies, studios, a bed and breakfast, non-profits and a weather forecasting business. The common thread is an owner who takes pride in their work and has a passion to see your company succeed.
