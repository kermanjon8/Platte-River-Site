One business per category

If you're the roofer on the card, you're the only roofer on the card. Once a category is taken for a round, it's closed until the next one.

I hold to this even when it means turning a paying business away. It's the main reason a spot on a shared card is worth having.

How a round works

From your first message to the card landing in mailboxes.

  1. You claim your categoryTell me what your business does. If the category is open for the next round, it's yours once the spot is paid.
  2. I design your spotSend me your logo and what you want to say, or your own finished ad. You approve a proof before anything prints.
  3. The card prints and mailsI handle printing and the postal paperwork, then take the cards to the post office myself.
  4. You can see the receiptI keep the stamped USPS receipt for every round, and I'll show it to you.
