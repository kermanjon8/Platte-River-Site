I won't promise a number of calls or sales from an ad. Nobody honest can.

What I will do is show you what was spent, how many people it reached, and what they did next, so you can decide for yourself whether it's worth continuing.

How ads management works

Every business is different, so I quote ads management after we talk.

  1. We set the goalCalls, messages, website visits, or foot traffic. One goal per campaign.
  2. I build the campaignThe audience, the ad itself, and a budget you're comfortable with.
  3. I watch itI check on it while it runs and change what isn't working.
  4. You get the numbersA plain summary of what was spent and what came of it.
