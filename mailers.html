Two ways to do it

Solo

The whole card is yours. Both sides, your message, nobody else on it. This is the one for a grand opening, a big sale, or anything you want a neighborhood to notice.

Semi-solo

You share the card with a small number of other businesses and split the cost. You get much more room than a spot on The Kearney Insider, for less than mailing a card by yourself.
